UX Architecture Note: Launch = handoff, not merge

Per IA-SPEC.md, SERAA operates as a discovery and entitlement layer above the products. Clicking into an entitled product passes the user's identity and org hint, then that product's own Scope-Based Access Control (SBAC) takes over its own session and permissions rather than merging authorization into a shared IAM graph prematurely.
